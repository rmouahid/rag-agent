# Rolling stock

Lumen Rail owns two families of trains, all maintained at the Saint-Ondine depot.

## Corvid electric multiple units

The 22 Corvid trains run the Amber and Cobalt lines. They were built by Brannock Works and entered service in 2014. Each Corvid has 4 cars and 236 seats, including 16 tip-up seats in the bicycle area, and reaches a top speed of 160 km/h. Every train has two accessible toilets and a ramp that the conductor deploys at each door.

## Heron diesel railcars

The 9 Heron railcars date from 1996, which makes them the oldest trains in the fleet. They run the non-electrified Juniper Line. A Heron has 2 cars and 112 seats and reaches 120 km/h. Two Herons are often coupled on ski trains in winter.

## Fleet renewal

In 2024, Lumen Rail ordered 8 battery-electric Corvid B trains from Brannock Works to replace the Heron railcars. They charge under the overhead wires between Port Severin and Castelmont and run on batteries on the Juniper Line, with a range of 190 km. Deliveries are planned for 2027, and the last Heron should be withdrawn in 2028.
