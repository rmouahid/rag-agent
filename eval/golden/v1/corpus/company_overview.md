# Lumen Rail — Company overview

Lumen Rail is the regional passenger railway operator of the Varennes Valley. The company was founded in 1998, when the regional council merged three local branch-line operators into a single public company. Its head office is in Castelmont, in the former goods shed next to Castelmont Central station.

## Key figures

Lumen Rail employs 1,240 people, of whom 410 are drivers and conductors. The network is 412 km long and serves 28 stations on three lines: the Amber Line, the Cobalt Line and the Juniper Line. In 2025, Lumen Rail carried 14.2 million passengers, up 6% on 2024.

## Governance

The company is owned by the Varennes Valley regional council. Its chief executive since 2021 is Ilse Marchetti, previously head of operations at the port of Port Severin. A supervisory board of nine members, four of them elected by employees, approves the yearly budget and the fleet plan.

## Strategy

The 2030 plan has two goals: running every line without diesel traction by 2030, and raising punctuality to 95% of trains arriving less than five minutes late (91.3% in 2025). The diesel railcars of the Juniper Line are the last ones in service; their replacement is described in the rolling stock document.
