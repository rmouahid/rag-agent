# Lumen Miles loyalty programme

Lumen Miles is the free loyalty programme of Lumen Rail. Members earn 1 mile for every euro spent on single and return tickets. Buying a Valley Pass earns 500 miles at once; the Flex Pass earns no miles.

## Status levels

- **Silver**: reached with 2,000 miles collected in a calendar year. Silver members get a 10% discount on single and return tickets.
- **Gold**: reached with 6,000 miles collected in a calendar year. Gold members get a 20% discount on single and return tickets and access to the lounge at Castelmont Central.

A status is kept until the end of the following calendar year.

## Using miles

Miles can be exchanged for tickets: 1,000 miles buy a single ticket on any line. Miles expire 24 months after they were earned. Members can transfer up to 3,000 miles a year to another member of the same household.
