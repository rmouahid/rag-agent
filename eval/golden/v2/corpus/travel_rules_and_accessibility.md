# Travel rules and accessibility

## Bicycles

Up to 4 bicycles are allowed per train, in the bicycle area. They travel free outside peak times; during weekday peak times a bicycle ticket costs €3. Folded bicycles count as luggage and are always free.

## Pets

Small pets in a closed basket travel free. Larger dogs pay a flat €5 per journey and must be kept on a lead and muzzled.

## Luggage

Each passenger may bring two large bags and one item of hand luggage. Skis are accepted on all trains and are stored in the bicycle area on ski trains.

## Accessibility

Passengers who need help to board can book assistance free of charge at least 4 hours before their journey, by phone or on the website. All stations have step-free access to the platforms except Brisac and Vellon, which are being rebuilt; until the end of the works, Lumen Rail offers a free accessible taxi from the nearest step-free station.

## Safety on board

In an emergency, passengers can call the on-board security team on 1414, a free number that works on every Lumen Rail train. Emergency handles are next to every door; they stop the train at the next station, not between stations, unless the conductor decides otherwise.
