# Tickets, fares and refunds

## Ticket types

- **Single ticket**: one journey, valid on the day of travel only.
- **Return ticket**: valid for the outward journey on the chosen date and for the return within 7 days.
- **Flex Pass**: unlimited travel on all three lines for one calendar month, for €89.
- **Valley Pass**: unlimited travel on all three lines for one year, for €790. Holders also get one free return ticket for a companion each month.

Children under 6 travel free. Young people aged 6 to 17 pay half price on single and return tickets. Passes have no reduced rate.

## Refunds

Standard single and return tickets are fully refunded if they are cancelled at least 24 hours before departure. Between 24 hours and the departure time, 50% of the price is refunded. No refund is given after departure. A Flex Pass can be refunded in full during its first 5 days of validity, and is not refundable after that. The Valley Pass is refunded pro rata for each full month left, minus a €30 handling fee.

Refunds are paid back to the original means of payment within 10 working days.

## Compensation for delays

When a train arrives late at the passenger's destination, Lumen Rail pays compensation as a share of the ticket price: 25% for a delay of 30 to 59 minutes, and 50% for a delay of 60 minutes or more. Claims must be made within 28 days of the journey, with the online form D-12 or at a ticket office. Pass holders receive a voucher of €5 for each delay of 60 minutes or more, capped at €40 a year.
