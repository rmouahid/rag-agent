# Lines and timetable

Lumen Rail runs three lines. Castelmont Central is shared by the Amber and Cobalt lines, and Port Severin by the Amber and Juniper lines, where passengers can change trains.

## Amber Line

The Amber Line links Castelmont Central to Port Severin along the river. It is 96 km long and serves 11 stations. A stopping train takes 1 h 05 min from end to end. On weekdays, trains run every 30 minutes at peak times (06:30–09:00 and 16:30–19:00) and every hour otherwise. The first train leaves Castelmont Central at 05:40 and the last one at 23:10.

## Cobalt Line

The Cobalt Line climbs from Castelmont Central to the mountain town of Hautval. It is 148 km long and serves 9 stations, including Saint-Ondine, where the fleet maintenance depot is located. The express service, which skips four stations, takes 1 h 50 min; stopping trains take 2 h 25 min. Trains run every hour from 06:10 to 21:10. The two stations of Brisac and Vellon, on this line, are being rebuilt until 2027.

## Juniper Line

The Juniper Line runs from Port Severin to the lake resort of Lac Ombre. It is 168 km long and serves 10 stations. The line is not electrified. Trains run every two hours from 07:15 to 19:15; from December to March, an extra ski train leaves Port Severin at 06:05 on Saturdays and Sundays. A journey from end to end takes 2 h 40 min.

## Disruptions

Planned engineering works are announced at least 14 days in advance on the Lumen Rail website and at stations. During works, replacement buses run to the same timetable, with a margin of up to 20 extra minutes.
